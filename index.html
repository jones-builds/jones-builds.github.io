// RAPID HAND
MARS LAB · PURDUE · DEC 2025 – PRESENT

RAPID Hand — Bring-Up of an Open-Source Robotic Hand

Assembled and brought up a 20-actuator open-source dexterous hand. Now teleoperating it with an Apple Vision Pro.

TTL bus · 4 Mbps · idle
  • Brought up 20 DYNAMIXEL XL330/XC330 servos, verifying IDs 0–19 on a 4 Mbps TTL bus via U2D2
  • Built Python diagnostics (DYNAMIXEL SDK) logging position, voltage, mode and hardware errors per actuator to CSV
  • Bench-tested position commands and documented coupling between two knuckle axes
  • Designed a 3D-printed calibration fixture and redesigned bevel-gear supports for 5 mm shafts to remove play

// CNC · MTCONNECT
KIRBY RISK PRECISION MACHINING · JAN – MAY 2026

Multi-Modal CNC Data Integration & Scrap Analysis

Fused machine-state and physical sensor data from a production CNC to test whether scrap could be predicted.

  • Aligned machine status, spindle load, current and acoustic signals across 1.3M+ MTConnect records and 891 cycles
  • Set a 17-minute cycle baseline across 3 part programs and analyzed 96 defects
  • Found too few examples per failure mode for reliable prediction — and caught label leakage from shift-level labels — and presented both limits openly to faculty and plant engineers
