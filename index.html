End-to-end pipeline predicting scrap on a production CNC by fusing machine-state and physical sensor data.

  • Trained XGBoost classifiers on the fused feature set
  • Diagnosed label leakage from shift-level labeling — the model was learning shift context, not tool-failure physics — and presented the limitation openly to faculty and industry engineers
