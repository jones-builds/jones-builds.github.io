Autonomous Water Trash-Collecting Robot

An autonomous surface robot that finds and collects floating debris, taken from sketches through prototypes and iteration.

Overview

Floating trash builds up in ponds, canals and harbors where it is hard to reach by hand. This project designed an autonomous surface robot that patrols the water and collects debris as it goes.

Design process

Ideation→Concept CAD→Prototype→Iterate→Results

What I did

Ideated

Explored concepts for a hull and collection mechanism that can gather floating debris without stalling the robot.

Modeled in CAD

Developed the chosen concept as a CAD model.

Prototyped and iterated

Built prototypes and refined the design based on what testing showed.
