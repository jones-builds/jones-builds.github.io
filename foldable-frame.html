Foldable Motorbike Frame

Designing a compact two-wheeler frame that folds for storage, and proving with FEA that it still carries the load.

Overview

A folding frame has to be compact when stored and stiff when ridden, and the hinge points that make folding possible are also where stress concentrates. This project designed a foldable two-wheeler frame and validated it with finite element analysis.

Process

Packaging constraints→CAD model→Static FEA→Dynamic FEA→Geometry optimization→Publication

What I did

Modeled the frame

Built the compact foldable frame in CAD around the folded-size constraints.

Ran the analysis

Validated the frame under static and dynamic loading in ANSYS.

Optimized the geometry

Used the von Mises stress results to refine the frame geometry while keeping it within the folding packaging limits.

Published the work

The design and analysis were published in the International Journal of Vehicle Structures & Systems (IJVSS) in 2024.

What I learned

Design constraints compete with each other. FEA was most useful as a design tool, showing where material could come out and where it had to stay, rather than only as a final pass/fail check.
