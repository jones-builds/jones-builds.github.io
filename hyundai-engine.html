Overview

Hyundai's Kappa engine line builds engines at automotive volume, where small inefficiencies multiply fast. My internship focused on how quality is checked along the line, where flow slows down, and how the robotic handling cells fit in.

What I did

Mapped the line

Evaluated 5 stages of Kappa engine assembly, identifying 12 quality checkpoints and 3 bottlenecks.

Verified components

Checked 200+ engine components against ISO/ASME drawings, dimensional tolerances and inspection criteria.

Studied the robotic cells

Observed ABB and KUKA robotic handling cells and how they connect to manual stations.

Recommended improvements

Delivered 8 process-improvement recommendations aligned with IATF 16949 automotive quality practice.

What I learned

On a real line, quality and throughput are the same problem. A checkpoint in the wrong place becomes a bottleneck, and a bottleneck pushes people to rush the check.
